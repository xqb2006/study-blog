# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is a personal blog owner working in a browser-based CMS to draft, edit, publish, organize, and rebuild blog content.

## Product Purpose

This CMS exists to make day-to-day blog operations quick, clear, and pleasant: review content health, manage posts, adjust site settings, organize taxonomy and media, and trigger builds without leaving the dashboard.

## Brand Personality

Soft, expressive, Sakura-themed. The interface should feel close to the Astro Koharu blog identity: pink, rounded, light, anime-influenced, and calm enough for repeated content work.

## Anti-references

Avoid dark cyber dashboards, neon HUD styling, generic SaaS purple gradients, heavy shadows, and admin panels that feel detached from the blog's cute Sakura/Koharu identity.

## Design Principles

- Keep the CMS recognizably tied to the public blog.
- Make the first screen useful for real publishing decisions.
- Use Sakura/pink styling as atmosphere, not as clutter.
- Preserve standard product UI affordances for tables, filters, settings, and actions.
- Keep dense management workflows readable on desktop and usable on mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for text and controls. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, responsive layout, and reduced-motion preferences.
